# FinNexa Admin

FinNexa is a professional, high-performance financial management and compliance platform designed for UK Limited Companies. It provides business owners with a unified dashboard to track expenditures, revenue, and statutory filing obligations with high-end aesthetics and mobile-first responsiveness.

## 🚀 Core Features

### 1. **Portfolio Management**
- **Company Dashboard:** A bird's-eye view of all registered entities in your portfolio.
- **Smart Onboarding:** Easily add and manage multiple companies with real-time sync to Companies House data via API.
- **Dormant Mode:** Specialized tracking for companies that are not currently trading.

### 2. **Financial Ledgers & Analytics**
- **Unified Summary:** A combined view of all expenses and revenue, allowing for a complete "General Ledger" experience.
- **Dynamic Analytics:** Visual pie charts providing instant category-based spend and income breakdowns.
- **Accountant's Pack:** One-click export of your entire financial history, company metadata, and statutory registers into a professional Excel format.
- **Real-time Revenue Tracking:** Automated syncing of paid invoices combined with manual revenue entry.

### 3. **Statutory Filing Center**
- **Compliance Roadmap:** Real-time calculation of filing deadlines for Accounts (including the complex first-year 21-month rule) and Confirmation Statements (CS01).
- **HMRC Integration:** Tracking for Corporation Tax registration and payment deadlines based on trading activity.
- **Filing History:** A complete archive of all statutory submissions logged through the platform.

### 4. **Statutory Books**
- **Register of Members:** Maintain a digital statutory register of shareholders.
- **Share Certificate Generation:** Instantly generate professional, print-ready share certificates for any share allotment or transfer.
- **Register of Directors:** Keep a clean, historical record of company appointments and resignations.

## 🎨 Design Philosophy
FinNexa is built with a **"Flat Professional"** aesthetic:
- **Unified Branding:** Consistent use of the **Inter** typeface and high-contrast typography.
- **Adaptive Theme:** Sophisticated Light Mode (`#FDFCF8`) and Deep Dark Mode (`#0A0D14`) support.
- **Mobile Responsive:** All charts, tables, and forms are meticulously optimized for perfect visibility on devices of all sizes.

## 🛠 Tech Stack
- **Frontend:** React (Vite), Tailwind CSS, Recharts (Data Visualization).
- **Backend:** Firebase (Firestore, Authentication, Hosting).
- **Date Engine:** Day.js (Custom logic for Companies House statutory period math).
- **Exports:** XLSX (SheetJS) for professional financial reporting.
